## Events and Promotions page (Scot's text copy, 2026-10-28)
- "There's always something sweet happening in Pelham!" Seasonal flavors, fresh handmade donuts, locally roasted coffee, and a little hometown fun.
- **Fall flavors & cozy coffee:** "Make Donut Joe's part of your fall routine… pick up something sweet for the office, or grab breakfast before a Saturday full of football." "Seasonal selections change, and availability varies daily. Come early for the best selection!"
- **Bring something sweet to your next gathering:** game-day get-togethers, office mornings and family celebrations; "Call ahead to discuss larger orders" (205-663-6006).
- **Keep up:** Facebook and Instagram for fresh-from-the-case updates and seasonal arrivals.
- **Come see us:** "beside the railroad tracks, under the pecan tree, just off I-65 at Exit 242—the tank exit." Tuesday–Saturday 6:00 AM–6:00 PM; **closed Sunday and Monday**.
- Sign-off: **"Handmade daily. Hometown since 2009."** Footer: "Visit us early — the best flavors go fast!"
