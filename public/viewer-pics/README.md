# The viewer's sample pictures (DP-14, V1c)

Pictures for the made-up stories and events in "See Your WayTuGo in Action" (public/viewer-sample.mjs). Each is
`<key>.jpg`, about 1200 × 800 (landscape), at least 300 px wide; a story or event whose picture isn't here shows as
text. When a picture is added, its key goes into `PICS` in viewer-sample.mjs.
