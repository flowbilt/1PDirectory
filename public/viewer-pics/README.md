# The viewer's sample pictures (DP-14, V1c)

Pictures for the made-up stories and events in "See Your WayTuGo in Action" (public/viewer-sample.mjs). Each is
`<key>.jpg`, about 1200 × 800 (landscape), at least 300 px wide; a story or event whose picture isn't here shows as
text. When a picture is added, its key goes into `PICS` in viewer-sample.mjs.

V5f (DP-15): real-life headlines and events in place of V1c's jokes (Scot: "Something that looks like real life news
headlines"). Made by Scot for 1Point (2026-11-20), without words (the screen writes the headline over the bottom), each
compressed to under 300 KB for phones.

| Key | For |
|---|---|
| `riverfront-park` | City Breaks Ground on Riverfront Park Expansion |
| `farmers-market` | Farmers Market Returns to Main Street for the Season |
| `office-occupancy` | Downtown Office Occupancy Climbs for Third Straight Quarter; also the building photo behind the directory |
| `airport-routes` | Regional Airport Adds Three Nonstop Routes |
| `food-bank` | Local Food Bank Tops Holiday Donation Goal |
| `robotics-team` | High School Robotics Team Heads to State Finals |
| `holiday-market` | Holiday Market at City Center Plaza (event) |
| `charity-5k` | Charity 5K Downtown (event) |
| `symphony-park` | Symphony in the Park (event) |

V1c's pictures (donuts, monkey, goat, squirrel, socks, office-plant, monster-trikes, chili, dog) are no longer used.
